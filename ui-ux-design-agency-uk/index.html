What UK software teams tend to need

⚠

UK GDPR shapes the interface, not just the backend

Consent, data minimisation and subject-access rights all surface as design decisions — how consent is requested, what is collected at signup, how a user exercises deletion. Treating this as purely a legal or engineering concern produces flows that technically comply and measurably underperform.

☷

Public-sector-adjacent work has a higher accessibility bar

The Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations set WCAG 2.2 AA as the requirement, and that expectation increasingly propagates to suppliers and to enterprise buyers generally. Retrofitting is far more expensive than designing to it.

▣

Senior design capacity is scarce and expensive

London senior product design salaries plus a lengthy hiring cycle mean many UK teams cannot get senior capacity onto a problem in the quarter it matters.

↻

Offshore has usually meant a handoff, not a collaboration

The common failure mode is layers and lag: account managers relaying requirements, and questions answered overnight. With a 4.5-hour offset and senior designers working directly with you, neither is structural.
