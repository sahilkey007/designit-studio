About Designit

India's premier B2B SaaS and product design agency. We design high-conversion digital experiences for startups and enterprise software companies worldwide.

What Designit Does

Designit is a product design agency based in Noida, India. The agency specialises in UI/UX design for B2B SaaS products, Fintech applications, edtech platforms, and enterprise software. Every engagement is led directly by the founder, Sahil Sharma.

Services include: UI/UX design, product design, branding, design systems, mobile app design, website redesign, conversion rate optimisation, and landing page design.
