About Designit

A global, research-led B2B SaaS and product design studio. We start with user research, then design high-conversion digital experiences for startups and enterprise software companies worldwide.

What Designit Does

Designit is a research-led product design studio based in Noida, India, serving clients worldwide. The studio specialises in UI/UX design for B2B SaaS products, Fintech applications, edtech platforms, and enterprise software. Every engagement starts with user research and is led by senior designers — no account managers, no layers.

Services include: UI/UX design, product design, branding, design systems, mobile app design, website redesign, conversion rate optimisation, and landing page design.

Our Team

Designit is led by a senior team with 5+ years of experience in B2B SaaS and e-commerce conversion design. Clients work directly with senior designers from research and discovery through to final developer handoff — no account managers, no layers.
