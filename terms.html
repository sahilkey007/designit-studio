3. Engagement Process

Our standard engagement follows this process:

  1. Discovery call — a no-obligation 30-minute call to understand your project and goals
  2. Proposal — a written proposal outlining scope, deliverables, timeline, and pricing, sent within 48–72 hours
  3. Agreement — written confirmation (email or signed document) from the Client
  4. Advance payment — 50% of the project fee paid before work begins
  5. Kickoff — project starts within 3–5 business days of cleared payment

No work begins until both written agreement and advance payment are received. Verbal instructions alone do not constitute an agreement.

4. Payment Terms

5. Revisions & Scope Changes

Our standard engagement includes 2–3 rounds of revisions per design stage as specified in the project proposal. Each revision round covers consolidated feedback — not sequential, single-comment changes.

What constitutes a revision

A revision round includes adjustments to layout, typography, colour, copy placement, or component sizing within the agreed design direction. It does not include changes to the fundamental concept, user flow, or scope of work.

Additional revisions

Revisions beyond the included rounds will be billed at our standard day rate as agreed in the proposal. We will always notify you before incurring additional charges.

Scope changes

Any request that falls outside the agreed SOW (new pages, additional features, additional research rounds, etc.) constitutes a scope change. Scope changes require a written amendment to the proposal and may affect the timeline and cost.
