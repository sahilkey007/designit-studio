How Much Does UI/UX Design Cost in India? (2026 Honest Guide)

Founders and product managers in India face a maddening problem when budgeting for design: the range of quotes they receive is enormous. One agency quotes ₹8,000 for a landing page. Another quotes ₹2,50,000. Both call themselves "UI/UX designers."

This guide cuts through the noise. After delivering design work for over 50 products — from funded SaaS startups to listed enterprises — I'm publishing the actual numbers, what drives the price, and where founders consistently overpay or underpay.

No vague ranges. Real figures.

Why Design Pricing in India Is So Confusing

Three completely different types of people call themselves "UI/UX designers" in India:

  1. Template resellers — buy a ThemeForest template, swap your logo, charge ₹5,000–25,000. You're paying for nothing.
  2. Junior visual designers — can execute screens they're shown, but cannot identify UX problems or design systems independently. Mid-range billing.
  3. Product designers — understand user psychology, business goals, technical constraints, and conversion. They design from first principles, not templates. Premium billing.

The price you pay depends entirely on which of these three you're actually getting — and most buyers don't know until the work lands.

Full Product Design (SaaS / App)

This is where the real work is. A product has flows, states, edge cases, components, and data density that a landing page doesn't. Poor design here = user churn.

| Scope | Price Range | Includes |

|---|---|---|

| MVP (10–15 core screens) | ₹1,50,000–2,50,000 | Flows, wireframes, hi-fi UI, component library, dev handoff |

| V2 / redesign (25–40 screens) | ₹3,00,000–5,00,000 | Full UX audit, revised IA, all screens, design system foundation |

| Enterprise / complex product | ₹5,00,000–12,00,000+ | Research, IA, design system, multiple flows, stakeholder reviews |

What "cheap" product design costs you: If you pay ₹40,000 for a 20-screen product design and the UX is broken, your developer charges you another ₹3–8 lakh to rebuild once users churn. The ₹40,000 decision destroyed ₹10 lakh.

What Makes the Price Go Up

These factors add 20–60% to any project estimate. Ask about them before getting a quote:

  • Multiple user roles (admin + user + vendor = 3× the flows to design)
  • Dark mode or multi-theme requirement
  • Custom animations or micro-interactions
  • UX research (user interviews, competitive audit)
  • Developer handoff documentation (annotated specs, component inventory)
  • Accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA)
  • Tight deadline (design in under 2 weeks typically adds 20–30% rush fee)

The Three Red Flags of Cheap Design Agencies

1. They quote without asking questions.

A real designer can't quote without understanding your product, users, and goals. A quote that arrives within 10 minutes of your inquiry is a template-based number. The work will be template-based too.

2. They show mostly mockup screenshots, not real shipped products.

Mockups on Dribbble shots with pixel-perfect gradients and no real constraints are easy. Ask: "Can you show me a product that was built from your designs?" If they can't, they've never operated in the real world.

3. They don't ask about business metrics.

Design that doesn't track to conversion, retention, or time-on-task is decoration. If an agency never asks "what does success look like in numbers?" — they're decorating, not designing.
