# SEO Keyword Map — designit.co.in

Product-design-first keyword reference. Use this when writing new pages,
blog posts, meta tags, or ad copy so terminology stays consistent across
the site instead of drifting toward generic "website design" language.

## 3. Geo + Service Keywords

- Product design agency India
- Product design agency USA
- Product design studio UK
- UX design agency Dubai UAE
- Remote product design team

Prefer **"product design agency/studio [geo]"** over "UI/UX design agency
[geo]" — reinforces product-first positioning, still carries the same
search volume for design-agency intent.

## 6. Content Gaps (no dedicated landing page yet — candidates for new pages)

- Design system development (service #2 has no standalone page)
- UX design audit & analysis (service #5 has no standalone page)
- UX research & discovery (service #4 has no standalone page)
- Digital prototyping & validation (service #6 has no standalone page)

Each of the 7 homepage services currently gets one card + one paragraph.
Only the industry verticals (Fintech, EdTech, SaaS) and a few geo pages
have dedicated landing pages. Building out standalone pages for the 4
services above is the fastest way to capture more long-tail search volume
without new blog content.
