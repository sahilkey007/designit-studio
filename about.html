Our Story

From one laptop to 5+ countries

Designit didn't start in a co-working space or some glossy VC-funded setup. It started in a small bedroom with just one laptop, a noisy fan, and a founding team who couldn't stop thinking about why so many good ideas fail.

So we helped early teams fix broken onboarding, redesign confusing UX, and turn messy ideas into real products. One founder at a time.

But now? Designit works with 500+ startups, helps teams launch faster MVPs, improve conversion, and build systems that scale. Our design now powers 100M+ users across 22 countries, from early-stage AI tools to Series B SaaS giants.

We still build like it's day one — fast, honest, human. Behind every screen is someone betting it all on an idea.

Empathy

Clarity
