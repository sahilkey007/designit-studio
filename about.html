Our Story

From One Laptop
to 5+ Countries

Designit was founded in 2019 with a simple belief: great design should be accessible to every ambitious team, not just those with deep pockets. We started as a freelance practice — one designer, one laptop, and a relentless drive to prove that thoughtful design could transform businesses.

Within a year, word spread. Startups and growing companies came to us because we treated their products like our own. We didn't just push pixels — we asked hard questions, challenged assumptions, and obsessed over outcomes.

Today, Designit is a full design studio serving clients across 12+ countries. Our work powers products used by over 10 million people every month. But the mission hasn't changed: help ambitious teams build products their users genuinely love.
