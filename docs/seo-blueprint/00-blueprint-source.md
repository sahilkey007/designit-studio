Designit Website Rebuild + SEO + AEO + GEO Blueprint

I would now treat this as a website transformation project, not simply a redesign.

The material you attached gives us three important inputs:

The 100X SEO operating system, which correctly frames the goal around qualified traffic, commercial intent, leads, authority, backlinks, AI visibility and revenue rather than rankings alone.
Your SE Ranking competitor keyword snapshot, which exposes the kinds of service, location, problem and informational queries appearing in the competitive search landscape. The three screenshot pages also contain a significant amount of noisy or outdated data, which means we should use it as a keyword-discovery source rather than blindly turning every row into a page.
Your live Search Console data, which tells us what Google is already associating with Designit today. The important pattern is that Designit has early visibility around SaaS, UX, branding, Fintech, EdTech, AI and design services, but several commercial pages are not yet capturing that relevance strongly.

There is one important data limitation: the connected Ahrefs workspace currently does not expose the broader competitor organic-keyword extraction needed for a full Ahrefs Content Gap report. I therefore have not invented search-volume, backlink or competitor-ranking numbers. The plan below combines the attached SE Ranking evidence, your Search Console data, the live competitor sites, your 100X operating system and current Google/OpenAI guidance.

7. Competitor analysis: what each competitor is doing that is strategically useful
TheFinch

TheFinch is doing several things worth learning from.

Their current solutions page combines a very explicit acquisition mechanism, including an instant project cost estimate, with a large service taxonomy, solutions, industries, AI-related content, a staged delivery process, case studies, outcomes and testimonials.

The particularly useful pieces are:

A. Problem-based services

They do not simply say:

"We design."

They expose specific things people are looking for:

Design Discovery
MVP Design
Full Product Design
SaaS Dashboard
Custom Websites
Custom Mobile Apps
E-commerce
Digital Product Solutions

Their service content then asks the buyer about symptoms such as unclear product vision, user needs, scaling challenges or dashboard usability.

What Designit should take

Create problem-aware service blocks:

Users are dropping off during onboarding.

→ SaaS UX audit

Your product has grown into a collection of inconsistent workflows.

→ Product redesign + design system

You are launching a new SaaS product and need to validate the experience.

→ Product discovery + MVP design

Your website gets traffic but does not explain the product clearly.

→ Website UX + conversion redesign

This is much stronger than listing capabilities.

B. Cost/qualification mechanism

A Designit equivalent could be:

Project Scope & Fit Assessment

Instead of copying "instant cost estimate", create something aligned with your premium positioning:

What are you building?
What problem are you solving?
What stage are you at?
Which platforms?
How complex is the product?
Do you need research?
Do you need design systems?
Do you need implementation support?
What's your timeline?

The output can be:

Recommended engagement: UX Audit / Discovery Sprint / Product Redesign / Full Product Design

This is also a lead qualification mechanism.

Do not expose invented price ranges simply to imitate a competitor.
