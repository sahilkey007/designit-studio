Overview

CRM Reimagined for Modern Teams

Kelp Global is a next-generation customer relationship platform designed to help sales teams manage leads, track deals, and gain actionable insights without context switching. We designed their complete product suite — from a Chrome browser plugin that surfaces contact intelligence in real-time, to Truenorth, an analytics dashboard for pipeline visibility, and a comprehensive Deals Module for end-to-end deal management. The design system was built for speed, clarity, and scale.
