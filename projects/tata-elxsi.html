Overview

Next-Gen Automotive HMI

Tata Elxsi is a global design and technology services company that partners with leading automotive OEMs to create next-generation in-vehicle experiences. We designed two critical HMI systems — a full infotainment interface and a digital instrument cluster — for connected vehicles. The design prioritized safety, glanceability, and minimal driver distraction while delivering a visually striking modern cockpit experience. Both systems were designed with dark-mode-first principles and adaptive layouts for different screen sizes.
