CASE STUDY

Keeping ADDA Home Simple And Approachable

How we redesigned ADDA 247's home experience to boost user onboarding, reduce friction, and create an intuitive learning platform for millions of students.

About Vaultik

ADDA 247 is India's leading test preparation platform serving millions of government exam aspirants. The platform needed a complete UX overhaul to simplify navigation, improve content discovery, and create a cohesive learning experience across web and mobile.


The existing interface was cluttered with too many entry points, causing decision paralysis. Students couldn't find relevant content quickly, leading to high bounce rates and poor engagement metrics.

Client
ADDA 247
Industry
EdTech / Online Learning
Services
UX Research, UI Design, Design System
Timeline
4 Weeks
Platform
iOS, Android, Web

The Problem

Students were overwhelmed by the home screen. With 15+ entry points competing for attention, the cognitive load was too high. Key metrics were suffering:

  • → 62% of new users didn't complete onboarding
  • → Average session duration was just 2.3 minutes
  • → Support tickets about navigation exceeded 200/week
THE SOLUTION

A Focused, Goal-Oriented Home

We restructured the entire information architecture around student goals. Instead of showing everything, we surfaced what matters: their active course, next scheduled test, and personalized recommendations.


The new design reduced entry points from 15 to 5, implemented progressive disclosure, and added contextual navigation that adapts based on the student's learning stage.

Design Process

Our process followed a rigorous 4-phase approach, ensuring every decision was backed by data and validated with real users.

PHASE 1

UX Research & Design Validation

We conducted 25 user interviews, analyzed 3 months of analytics data, and ran a comprehensive heuristic evaluation. Key findings shaped every design decision.

Card sorting exercises with 40 participants helped us restructure the navigation taxonomy, reducing the average task completion time by 40%.

Visual Identity and Brand Tiles

The refreshed visual system maintains ADDA 247's brand recognition while introducing cleaner typography, improved contrast ratios, and a more spacious layout.

Design System

A comprehensive design system with 80+ components, ensuring consistency across all platforms and enabling the dev team to ship 2x faster.

UI Design

The final UI balances visual appeal with functional clarity. Every element serves a purpose, every interaction feels natural.

RESULTS & OUTCOMES

Results & Outcomes

The redesign delivered measurable improvements across all key metrics within the first 30 days of launch.

45% Increase in onboarding completion
60% Reduction in support tickets
3.2x Increase in session duration
