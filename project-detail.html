Adda 247

Redesigning India's leading test preparation platform to simplify learning, boost engagement, and create a seamless experience for millions of government exam aspirants.

Overview

Adda 247 is India's largest test preparation platform, serving millions of students preparing for government examinations. The platform needed a complete UX overhaul to simplify navigation, improve content discovery, and create a cohesive learning experience across web and mobile. We partnered with the Adda 247 team to rethink the entire product experience from the ground up -- restructuring information architecture, building a scalable design system, and designing intuitive interfaces that reduce cognitive load while maximizing learning outcomes.

The Challenge

The Problem

Students were overwhelmed by the home screen. With 15+ entry points competing for attention, the cognitive load was too high. New users struggled to find relevant courses, navigate between test series and live classes, and track their progress. The existing interface suffered from inconsistent patterns, cluttered layouts, and a lack of clear hierarchy -- resulting in 62% of new users dropping off before completing onboarding, an average session duration of just 2.3 minutes, and over 200 navigation-related support tickets per week.

Our Approach

The Solution

We restructured the entire information architecture around student goals. Instead of showing everything at once, we surfaced what matters most: the active course, next scheduled test, and personalized recommendations. The new design reduced entry points from 15 to 5, implemented progressive disclosure patterns, and introduced contextual navigation that adapts based on the student's learning stage. A comprehensive design system ensured consistency across all touchpoints while enabling the engineering team to ship updates 2x faster.

Subproject 01

Test Series

We redesigned the test series experience to help students discover, purchase, and take mock tests with minimal friction. The new interface features a clean card-based layout with clear categorization by exam type, difficulty level indicators, and progress tracking -- making it effortless for students to find and resume their practice sessions.

Subproject 02

Live Class

The live class module was rebuilt from scratch to create an engaging, distraction-free learning environment. We introduced a streamlined schedule view, real-time class status indicators, and a seamless transition from browsing to joining live sessions. The redesigned interface also includes note-taking integration, doubt resolution features, and class recordings for students who miss live sessions.

Subproject 03

iOS & iPad App

We designed a native iOS and iPad application that leverages platform-specific patterns and capabilities. The app features adaptive layouts optimized for different screen sizes, split-view support on iPad for multitasking during study sessions, and native gesture interactions. Special attention was given to offline mode, allowing students to download content and practice tests for use in areas with limited connectivity.
